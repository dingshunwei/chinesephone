Chinese phone brands, in one page

Chinese smartphone brands cover almost every price point and form factor: camera flagships, foldables, gaming phones and value models. One-line notes on each brand, plus the questions we get asked most, are below — tap any line to expand.

Brands at a glance
  • HUAWEI — Flagship imaging and the HarmonyOS ecosystem; Mate and P-series lead on camera hardware.
  • Xiaomi — Value-to-flagship line-up plus a smart-home ecosystem tied together by HyperOS.
  • OPPO — Camera-first flagships and fast-charging technology; Find and Reno are the main lines.
  • vivo — Imaging-focused flagships running OriginOS; X and V series are the best known.
  • HONOR — Independent since 2020; strong foldables (Magic V) and competitive mid-range models.
  • OnePlus — Performance-first flagships with OxygenOS and a long fast-charging heritage.
  • realme — Youth- and value-oriented brand; GT series targets gaming performance.
  • Meizu — Design-led phones with the Flyme interface; a smaller, polished line-up.
  • Redmi — Xiaomi's value sub-brand: large batteries, durable builds, aggressive pricing.
  • iQOO — vivo's performance and gaming line, with fast charging as a headline feature.
  • nubia — Gaming phones under the RedMagic name: active cooling and shoulder triggers.
What “Made in China phones” means here

“Made in China phones” here means handsets designed and manufactured by Chinese brands — HUAWEI, Xiaomi, OPPO, vivo, HONOR, OnePlus, realme, Meizu, Redmi, iQOO and nubia. Most of them ship both a China-market version and a global version: the global one usually adds multi-band LTE/5G and Google services, while the China version may use a different band set and software build. Two phones with the same name can therefore differ in bands, ROM and charger — check the band list and the ROM variant first.

Brands also specialise differently: imaging (HUAWEI, vivo, OPPO), value and ecosystem (Xiaomi, Redmi, realme), performance and gaming (OnePlus, iQOO, nubia/RedMagic), and foldables (HONOR Magic V, HUAWEI Mate X). Open a brand card above to watch its devices.

Are Chinese phone brands available outside China?

Yes. Several brands sell global models in Europe, Asia and Latin America; others are mainly sold in China and are widely available through import channels. Global models normally include Google services and broader band support.

Do these phones support Google Play?

Global models ship with Google Play. China-market models usually do not, though Play can often be installed manually. Check the ROM variant before buying.

How do I check whether a model works with my carrier?

Compare the model’s supported LTE/5G bands with your carrier’s bands, then check the ROM (global or China). Send us the exact model and we will check it for you.

Why do two phones with the same name have different specs?

Brands often reuse a name across markets with different bands, memory options, chargers or software. The model code (international vs China SKU) is the reliable identifier.
